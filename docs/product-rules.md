# Core product rules

These twelve rules must never be broken. For each one, this page lists where it is enforced and which
tests prove it. Wherever possible a rule is enforced by the **database**, so it holds for every
client: the website, `/api/v1`, a future app, or a bug in server code. The page and server checks on
top of that give people clear messages.

| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Only registered businesses can participate                                 | Every business row needs an owner account with the business role (`businesses_check_owner_role`). Bookings are refused unless the business is approved, taking bookings, and its owner's account is active (`bookings_business_open` trigger)                                                                                  | `product_rules.test.sql`, `access_control.test.sql`                                                                                        |
| 2   | Only approved, active businesses appear in AI recommendations              | `match_businesses` and `concierge_provider_details` return approved businesses with an active owner only. Row level security hides the rest from customers and visitors. The concierge can show only providers its tools returned                                                                                              | `matching_engine.test.sql`, `access_control.test.sql`, `injection.test.ts` ("can't be talked into showing a business that isn't approved") |
| 3   | The AI searches the internal marketplace database only                     | The concierge has four tools (search, details, compare, reply), all of which read the platform database. It has no web search, no browsing and no SQL (`src/lib/concierge/tools.ts`)                                                                                                                                           | `injection.test.ts` ("has only the four concierge tools"), `e2e/suites/concierge.mjs`                                                      |
| 4   | The AI never invents businesses, prices, ratings, availability or bookings | Every reply is checked before anyone sees it (`src/lib/concierge/guard.ts`). Provider cards, prices, ratings and review counts must match what the tools returned. Availability claims need a checked date. Business names must be the platform's own. A refused reply is retried, then replaced with a safe answer and logged | `guard.test.ts`, `agent.test.ts`                                                                                                           |
| 5   | The AI Concierge handles discovery and matching                            | The concierge searches, compares and starts a booking request with real services (`src/lib/concierge`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | `agent.test.ts`, `rules.test.ts`, `e2e/suites/concierge.mjs`                                                                               |
| 6   | After confirmation, customer and business talk directly                    | A conversation opens when a booking is confirmed (`bookings_sync_conversation`). Only the two people in it can read or send (`check_message_sender` and row level security)                                                                                                                                                    | `chat.test.sql`, `e2e/suites/chat.mjs`                                                                                                     |
| 7   | The AI does not take part in customer–business chat                        | `messages` has no AI sender, and the concierge has no tool that reads or writes chats. AI conversations live in separate tables (`ai_conversations`, `ai_messages`)                                                                                                                                                            | `chat.test.sql`, `injection.test.ts`                                                                                                       |
| 8   | The platform controls the communication infrastructure                     | Chat runs on the platform (Supabase Realtime). Admins can restrict a chat or hide messages. Phone numbers, emails and outside links are refused in booking notes and quotes, and marked for the team in chat (`messages.shares_contact`)                                                                                       | `product_rules.test.sql`, `chat.test.sql`, `e2e/suites/api.mjs`                                                                            |
| 9   | Payment status is verified server-side                                     | A booking is confirmed only after the payment provider confirms the payment, through a signed webhook or a server check. Amount and currency must match the booking (`payments_check_booking`). Clients can't write payments or booking statuses                                                                               | `payments.test.sql`, `duplicates.test.sql`, `src/lib/payments/*.test.ts`, `e2e/suites/payments.mjs`, `e2e/suites/api.mjs`                  |
| 10  | Businesses don't get the customer's private contact details by default     | Businesses never read a customer's `users` row (email, phone). The street address is hidden until the booking is confirmed: clients can't read `bookings.address_line`, and `booking_address()` returns it to the business only once confirmed. Contact details are refused in booking notes                                   | `product_rules.test.sql`, `access_control.test.sql`, `e2e/suites/api.mjs`                                                                  |
| 11  | Only completed bookings can generate verified reviews                      | `reviews_check_booking` accepts a review only from the booking's customer, after the booking is completed, and only one per booking                                                                                                                                                                                            | `reviews.test.sql`, `e2e/suites/reviews.mjs`                                                                                               |
| 12  | Admins manage the marketplace, and sensitive actions are logged            | Every admin action writes to `admin_actions`, which is append-only. Opening a private chat is logged with the reason. The admin dashboard is at a private, unlinked URL                                                                                                                                                        | `access_control.test.sql`, `admin_dashboard.test.sql`, `e2e/suites/admin.mjs`                                                              |

## What can't be fully automated

- **Rule 4.** The guard checks every figure and provider card against the tool results, and business
  names against the platform's list. A made-up name that sounds like a business but isn't on the
  platform can't be detected reliably in free text. That doesn't create a booking path, though:
  customers can only open or book providers from the cards, and the cards come from the database.
- **Rule 8.** Chat messages with contact details are marked rather than blocked, so customers and
  businesses can still share details they need after confirmation (for example, a gate code or a
  landmark). Admins see the mark when they review a reported or disputed chat.

## Changing a rule

A change to any of these rules needs a database test in `supabase/tests/product_rules.test.sql` (or
the area's own test file) and an update to this page.
